# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want murmur's community — issues, pull requests, discussions — to be welcoming and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussion and review.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on the code and the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussion, or bad-faith arguing.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, code review, and any other
communication channel used for the project — and to anyone representing the project in
public spaces.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported by emailing **denys@malykhin.dev**. Reports will be handled
promptly and in confidence. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues/PRs, or ban
contributors whose behavior violates this code of conduct, at their discretion.
